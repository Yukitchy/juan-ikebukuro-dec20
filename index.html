01
Manga drawing class

MANGA DOJO TOKYO

13:00 · BOOKED

漫画道場

A manga studio where a professional teacher shows you how real manga is drawn: G-pens, ink and screentones. Your class is booked for 13:00 and already paid for, and the studio is expecting you. You take your finished page home.

02
Anime goods, manga, art supplies

ANIMATE IKEBUKURO

10:00–21:00

アニメイト池袋本店

Animate’s flagship store, recognised by Guinness World Records as the world’s largest anime shop. Nine floors of manga, character goods, games and cards, and proper manga art supplies for Nicholas. Five minutes on foot from the East Exit.

03
Pokémon cards and goods

POKÉMON CENTER MEGA TOKYO

10:00–20:00

ポケモンセンターメガトウキョー

Tokyo’s big Pokémon store, on the 2nd floor of Sunshine City (alpa). Plush, goods and the Pokémon Card Station for Japanese cards. It can be busy on Sundays, so we go in the afternoon.

04
One Piece only

MUGIWARA STORE IKEBUKURO

10:00–20:00

ONE PIECE 麦わらストア池袋店

The official One Piece shop, with more than 10,000 items and goods sold only here. It is on the same floor of Sunshine City as the Pokémon Center, so no extra walking.

05
Capsule toys

GASHAPON DEPARTMENT STORE IKEBUKURO

10:00–21:00

ガシャポンのデパート池袋総本店

One of the world’s largest capsule toy floors: more than 3,000 machines, on the 3rd floor of Sunshine City’s World Import Mart building. Bring 100-yen coins; there are change machines inside.
